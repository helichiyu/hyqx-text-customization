Abstract: We study noise-robust training for small corpora. Our method combines denoising rules with model-based filtering.
Keywords: denoising; small corpus; pretraining
1. Introduction
Small datasets degrade pretrained model quality. Section 2 describes our pipeline.
2. Methods
Rule filters remove layout noise; a unified gateway judges fluency per sample.
3. Experiments
We evaluate on three benchmarks; error rates drop by 18%.
4. Conclusion
The combined pipeline is effective for low-resource pretraining.
